Overview

I am an urban and regional planning graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). My research is on urban air quality: how PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀ levels in Khulna City change through the year, and how weather and regional air transport shape those changes.

My undergraduate thesis combined hourly monitoring data with NOAA-HYSPLIT trajectories, and a co-authored manuscript from it is under editorial assessment. During my internship at CEGIS, I mapped air quality across Fakirhat Upazila using Landsat 8 imagery and GIS.

From September 2025 to September 2026 I worked as a Young Professional at Rupantar on the GO4IMPact project in Satkhira, where I conducted community vulnerability assessments and supported Union Parishad Five-Year Plans. I am now applying for graduate study.

Research Projects

Undergraduate Thesis

Assessing the Temporal Dynamics of PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀ in Khulna City with Meteorological and Trajectory-based Analysis

Nov 2024 – Sept 2025 · Supervisor: Sharfan Upaul

Examined how PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀ levels in Khulna changed through 2024 using hourly data from the Department of Environment CAMS-8 station and NOAA-HYSPLIT trajectories, with Pearson correlation, multiple linear regression, K-Means clustering, and STL decomposition. Found winter and post-monsoon peaks of about 155 µg/m³ for PM₂.₅ and 220 µg/m³ for PM₁₀ against 15-40 µg/m³ in the monsoon, with regression models reaching R² of 0.79-0.91.

Meteorological and HYSPLIT-Derived Transport Influences on PM₂.₅ Exposure in a Khulna City, Bangladesh

Nov 2025 – Mar 2026 · Sharfan Upaul, Anika Tabassum (second author), Mahmudul Hasan

Assessed how weather and regional air transport influence PM₂.₅ variability in Khulna using hourly 2024 monitoring data, NASA POWER and NCEI meteorological data, and NOAA-HYSPLIT backward trajectories, with regression, Random Forest, K-Means clustering, and STL decomposition in Python. Found winter PM₂.₅ (~121 µg/m³) nearly eight times the WHO daily guideline against ~15 µg/m³ in the monsoon, with Random Forest explaining 64% of daily variance.

CEGIS Internship Research

Spatial and Temporal Analysis of Air Quality Using Remote Sensing and GIS in Fakirhat Upazila

Oct 2024

Mapped air quality across Fakirhat Upazila using Landsat 8 imagery (2024), ArcGIS, IDW interpolation, and AQI mapping, extending data from only three ground stations. Found PM₂.₅ was the only pollutant in the moderate AQI category, while vegetation (38.9%) and water bodies (28.7%) appeared to buffer pollution, and surface temperature had a weak effect (R² < 0.04 for most).

Academic Projects

Course projects from KUET. All four were group projects.

Noise Mapping

Mapping Noise Pollution in Khulna City: GIS Analysis and Correlation of Sound Levels, Traffic Volume, and Speed for Urban Planning Insights

URP 3232, Transportation Planning Studio · Group of five

We measured sound level, traffic volume and vehicle speed at 30 road sections in Khulna City during the morning peak, off-peak and evening peak, then mapped the sound levels in GIS. On the road, sound level rose with traffic volume (r = 0.697) and fell as speed increased (r = -0.823).

My part: Field survey, introduction, literature review, abstract and report outline.

Traffic Survey

A Comprehensive Analysis on Traffic Survey from Daulatpur to Goalkhali

URP 3232, Transportation Planning Studio · Group of five

We surveyed the Daulatpur to Goalkhali road in Khulna to assess its traffic flow and deficiencies. The work covered cross-sectional elements, traffic volume, spot speed, headway, intersections, a moving observer survey and parking, with the analysis done in MS Excel.

My part: Took part in every survey; wrote the introduction and the methodology for cross-sectional elements and parking; prepared the study area GIS map and photo map; compiled the final report.
